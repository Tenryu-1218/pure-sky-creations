青天閣 Pure Sky Creations

Welcome to Pure Sky Creations. I work across jewelry, metalsmithing, ink painting, calligraphy, seal carving, whatever catches my eye.  At the intersection of Eastern aesthetic tradition and Western fabrication craft. Precise in execution, narrative in form.

i. Jewelry & Metalsmithing

Sterling silver, designed and fabricated from sketch to finished piece.

Bezel and prong setting, mixed-metal construction, and sculptural casting — with labradorite, dendritic opal, amethyst, mystic topaz, ruby, and emerald as recurring stones.

Teardrop Kitten Series

One silhouette, many stones.

A signature seated-cat pendant — an oval head framing a single teardrop cabochon — cast again and again in different stones, each with its own character.

ii. Sumi-e Ink Painting

Original narrative compositions in the East Asian sumi-e tradition.

Brush, ink, and selective watercolor on paper and fabric — wet and dry brushwork, wash gradation, and gestural line. All subject matter is original.

iii. Shodo Calligraphy

Japanese brush calligraphy — single-take, performance and object at once.

Kanji and Chinese characters rendered in gyosho and related scripts at gallery scale.

Exhibited Heike Monogatari opening verse — Bloomfield Birmingham Art Center Student Showcase.

iv. Origami

Folded by hand.

A lighter practice — animals, birds, and creatures folded from single sheets. Studies in patience between the larger work.

02 — Commissions

Made to order.

Bespoke pieces created in dialogue with the person who will carry them — from a single stone or name to a finished object. A selection of past commissions.

JewelrySun & Moon — a couple's pair

HankoFour name seals, carved by hand

A hanko 判子 is a personal name seal carved into a small block of stone. Inked in vermilion and pressed to paper, it stands in for a handwritten signature across much of East Asia. Each one here is cut by hand, rendering a name in characters chosen for both their sound and their meaning.

Shodo CalligraphyCustom brushwork, commissioned to order

Commissioned shodō 書道 — a name, a word, or a line of verse brushed by hand in a chosen script, then sealed. New commissions are open.

大太刀 · Ōdachi · Seki

I travelled to Seki City — home to generations of Japanese bladesmiths — and, together with the smiths there, forged an ōdachi: the great long sword. What follows is the blade, step by step, from iron sand to finished edge.

04 — Martial Arts

The discipline of the body.

Bunbu Ryōdō · 文武両道 · "Văn võ kiêm toàn" — the harmonious balance of the pen and the sword.

A Japanese philosophical concept: the same discipline that guides the brush guides the body. A parallel practice, trained with the same attention to form, patience, and intention as the work made by hand.
